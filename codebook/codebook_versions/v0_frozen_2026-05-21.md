# Coronial Recommendation Coding Guide — v0 . Categories *will* shift after the first inter-rater
reliability round; this version exists to anchor the discussion.

**Unit of analysis:** A single recommendation as extracted from a coronial
finding. A finding with three numbered recommendations contributes three rows.

**Coding decision:** Each recommendation receives one label per axis (single-label,
not multi-label, in v0). If a recommendation appears to legitimately span two
categories on an axis, choose the *dominant* one and flag the case with the
`ambiguous_<axis>` boolean for later review.

---

## Axis 1 — Theme

*What is the recommendation about?* Bootstrapped from the 24 BERTopic topics
already derived in the upstream coronial-NLP pipeline; this codebook will compress
those to a stable, mutually-exclusive set of 8–12 themes once inter-rater
reliability has been piloted.

| Code | Label | Definition | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| THEME-MH | Mental health & suicide prevention | Screening, follow-up, crisis services, involuntary treatment. | "All EDs should have 24-hour psychiatric liaison." |
| THEME-CUST | Custody & detention | Police/corrections custody safety. | "CCTV monitoring of all cells holding intoxicated detainees." |
| THEME-MED | Healthcare delivery | Clinical care, medication safety, hospital systems. | "Mandatory medication reconciliation on admission." |
| THEME-RTA | Road & transport safety | Road, rail, aviation, maritime. | "Lower speed limit on the Hume Highway between X and Y." |
| THEME-DV | Domestic & family violence | Intimate-partner violence response. | "Police to attend all reported breaches of ADVO." |
| THEME-DRUG | Substance use | Illicit drugs, alcohol, prescribing. | "Pill-testing pilots at music festivals." |
| THEME-CHILD | Child protection | Child welfare and out-of-home care. | "FACS to review case plan within 14 days of any reported absconding." |
| THEME-WORK | Workplace safety | Occupational health and safety. | "SafeWork NSW to issue guidance on confined-space entry." |
| THEME-OTHER | Other / cross-cutting | Recommendations that do not fit the above. | (use sparingly; flag for review) |

## Axis 2 — Mechanism

*What kind of action is the recommendation calling for?*

| Code | Label | Definition | Example trigger phrases |
|---|---|---|---|
| MECH-LEG | Legislative / regulatory | Changing a law, regulation, code of practice. | "amend the Act", "regulations should require" |
| MECH-PROC | Procedural | New or modified policy, protocol, guideline, SOP. | "review the policy", "issue guidance" |
| MECH-TRAIN | Training & education | Staff or public education. | "training program", "curriculum should include" |
| MECH-FUND | Funding / resourcing | Additional staff, money, equipment. | "additional funding", "increase staffing" |
| MECH-INFRA | Infrastructure / equipment | Physical infrastructure or technology. | "install CCTV", "replace beds" |
| MECH-INV | Investigation / inquiry | A further inquiry, audit, or review. | "review should be undertaken", "audit" |
| MECH-OTHER | Other | Falls outside the above. | |

---

## Axis 3 — Addressee

*Who is being directed to act?* Code the **most-specific named addressee**; do
not infer.

| Code | Label | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| ADDR-DEPT-NAMED | Named government department | "NSW Health", "Department of Communities and Justice". |
| ADDR-DEPT-GENERIC | Government (generic) | "The government", "the State". |
| ADDR-HEALTH | Health service / LHD / hospital | A named health service or hospital. |
| ADDR-REG | Regulator | TGA, AHPRA, SafeWork, EPA, etc. |
| ADDR-PROF | Professional body / college | RACGP, ANMF, Law Society. |
| ADDR-POLICE | Police / corrections | Police force, Corrective Services. |
| ADDR-COURT | Coroner's court / judiciary | The coroner's court itself or other courts. |
| ADDR-PRIVATE | Private body | Named company, NGO, school. |
| ADDR-UNSPEC | Unspecified | No clear addressee. |

---

## Axis 4 — Specificity

*How actionable is the recommendation?* A 3-point ordinal scale.

| Code | Label | Definition | Diagnostic |
|---|---|---|---|
| SPEC-3 | Specific & measurable | Identifies the action, the actor, and either a deadline or a measurable outcome. | "By 1 July, X shall implement Y to achieve Z." |
| SPEC-2 | Specific (no metric) | Identifies action and actor but no deadline / outcome metric. | "X should review its policy on Y." |
| SPEC-1 | Aspirational | Names a goal or principle without binding any actor or action. | "Greater coordination across agencies is needed." |

---

## Axis 5 — Scope

*Is the recommendation addressing the individual case or proposing systemic reform?*

| Code | Label | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| SCOPE-IND | Individual | Action localised to a single facility, patient, or family. |
| SCOPE-SYS | Systemic | Action intended to apply across a population, sector, or jurisdiction. |
| SCOPE-MIX | Mixed | Genuine span of both — use sparingly; flag for review. |

---

## Flags

In addition to the five axis labels, each row carries optional booleans:

- `ambiguous_<axis>` — coder was torn between two categories on this axis.
- `requires_legal_expertise` — meaningful interpretation requires legal training.
- `non_english_or_redacted` — text contains substantial non-English content or redactions.

## Appendix — Unlabelled real-corpus snippets

The examples in the per-axis tables above are **invented** for illustration.
The snippets below are **real** recommendations from the Australian corpus,
selected for jurisdictional diversity and prose-style variety. They are
**deliberately unlabelled**: if a coder reads a real snippet here *with*
a label already attached, that label anchors the coder's judgement on
similar items during the IRR pilot. The appendix is here so coders can
calibrate to real prose, not to consult worked answers.

Names of deceased persons and individual professionals have been cropped
out of these excerpts.

> *(NSW)* "1. That the Office of Sport consider working with NSW Karate
> (and possibly other martial arts organisations) to develop a general
> head injury/concussion protocol or policy for the use of karate
> organisations at gradings and training. This process should take place
> with expert medical advice."

> *(WA)* "I recommend telephone calls recorded prior to the death of a
> prisoner in custody be provided to the coroners court as they were in
> the past. All prison deaths are mandated to be inquested, and while I
> appreciate not all telephone calls, although recorded are listened to
> within the prison system, nor is it feasible, they may provide some
> insight, post the event, as to what may have been in operation in the
> prisoner's mind at the time."

> *(ACT)* "I recommend that the ACT Government amend section 43 of the
> Health Act 1993 to avoid a repetition of this conduct."

> *(SA)* "Pursuant to Section 25(2) of the Coroner's Act 1975, I recommend
> that General Medical Practitioners be reminded that, when a patient has
> been referred to a specialist, it is necessary to communicate clearly
> with that specialist before prescribing medication or providing other
> forms of treatment which may be relevant to treatment being provided by
> the specialist."

> *(VIC)* "Pursuant to section 72(2) of the Coroners Act 2008, I make the
> following recommendation(s) connected with the death: 1. That the Public
> Advocate review OPA policy in relation to the scope of exercise of
> guardianship powers in particular in relation to the level of
> supervision and follow up of delivery of medical treatment."

Notice the prose patterns: explicit statute citations ("Pursuant to
Section 25(2)..."), numbered list markers, embedded justification
clauses, mixed addressee specificity (named body in NSW vs. "General
Medical Practitioners" generally in SA), and varied mechanism mix
(legislative in ACT vs. procedural in NSW vs. training-adjacent in SA).
