# Coronial Recommendation Coding Guide — v1.0

**Status:** Working version, post v0 pilot. Changes from v0 reflect the inspection of THEME-OTHER and MECH-OTHER rows from the n=50 v0 pilot.

**Unit of analysis:** A single recommendation as extracted from a coronial finding. A finding with three numbered recommendations contributes three rows.

**Coding decision:** Each recommendation receives one label per axis (single-label, not multi-label). If a recommendation appears to legitimately span two categories on an axis, choose the *dominant* one and flag the case with the `ambiguous_<axis>` boolean for later review.

---

## Axis 1 — Theme

*What is the recommendation about?* Twelve mutually-exclusive categories (was 9 in v0). The three new categories (`THEME-REC`, `THEME-FIRE`, `THEME-PROD`) were added after v0-pilot inspection showed 8 of 19 `THEME-OTHER` rows fit these patterns. `THEME-CUST` has been broadened to encompass police use of force.

| Code | Label | Definition | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| THEME-MH | Mental health & suicide prevention | Screening, follow-up, crisis services, involuntary treatment, suicide prevention messaging. | "All EDs should have 24-hour psychiatric liaison." |
| THEME-CUST | Custody, detention, and police use of force | In-custody deaths (police, corrections, immigration); restraint techniques; Tasers; police-family interactions during fatal encounters. | "CCTV monitoring of all cells holding intoxicated detainees." "Review of Taser deployment protocols." |
| THEME-MED | Healthcare delivery | Clinical care, medication safety, hospital systems, ambulance services, vaccine information. | "Mandatory medication reconciliation on admission." |
| THEME-RTA | Road & transport safety | Road, rail, aviation, maritime *transport*. (Recreational water use is `THEME-REC`.) | "Lower speed limit on the Hume Highway between X and Y." |
| THEME-DV | Domestic & family violence | Intimate-partner violence response. | "Police to attend all reported breaches of ADVO." |
| THEME-DRUG | Substance use | Illicit drugs, alcohol, prescribing-related harm. | "Pill-testing pilots at music festivals." |
| THEME-CHILD | Child protection | Child welfare, out-of-home care, mandatory reporting. | "FACS to review case plan within 14 days of any reported absconding." |
| THEME-WORK | Workplace safety | Occupational health and safety. | "SafeWork NSW to issue guidance on confined-space entry." |
| THEME-REC | Recreational & water safety | Water sports, boating, swimming, river safety, drownings outside vehicles, beach safety, outdoor recreation. *New in v1.0.* | "Develop public education programs about the dangers of inland river water-skiing." |
| THEME-FIRE | Fire & structural safety | Smoke detectors, building code, evacuation, residential fire risk, structural safety of dwellings. *New in v1.0.* | "Smoke detectors should be mandatory in informal sleeping spaces." |
| THEME-PROD | Product, chemical & poison safety | Regulation of dangerous substances, consumer products, chemicals, poisons; product recalls. *New in v1.0.* | "Restrict retail sale of concentrated potassium cyanide." |
| THEME-OTHER | Other / cross-cutting | Use only when none of the above fits. Flag for review. | (use sparingly) |

### Decision rules

- **Maritime transport (THEME-RTA) vs. recreational water (THEME-REC).** A commercial ferry sinking is RTA; a family in a tinnie capsizing while fishing is REC.
- **Custody (THEME-CUST) vs. mental health (THEME-MH).** If a death occurs in custody but the recommendation targets mental-health screening of detainees, prefer the *intervention* axis: a screening recommendation is MH, a cell-design recommendation is CUST.
- **Healthcare delivery (THEME-MED) vs. mental health (THEME-MH).** MED is for the *delivery system*; MH is for *psychiatric / suicide-specific* content. A general ED triage recommendation is MED; an ED psychiatric-liaison recommendation is MH.

## Axis 2 — Mechanism

*What kind of action is the recommendation calling for?* Unchanged from v0.

| Code | Label | Definition | Example trigger phrases |
|---|---|---|---|
| MECH-LEG | Legislative / regulatory | Changing a law, regulation, code of practice. | "amend the Act", "regulations should require" |
| MECH-PROC | Procedural | New or modified policy, protocol, guideline, SOP. | "review the policy", "issue guidance" |
| MECH-TRAIN | Training & education | Staff or public education. | "training program", "curriculum should include" |
| MECH-FUND | Funding / resourcing | Additional staff, money, equipment. | "additional funding", "increase staffing" |
| MECH-INFRA | Infrastructure / equipment | Physical infrastructure or technology. | "install CCTV", "replace beds" |
| MECH-INV | Investigation / inquiry | A further inquiry, audit, or review. | "review should be undertaken", "audit" |
| MECH-OTHER | Other | Falls outside the above. Includes *exhortations* / observations without a specific named mechanism (e.g. "I urge X to ..." with no specific action). | "highlights the importance of"; "I urge" |

### Decision rules

- **Mechanism is what is being *recommended*, not what already happened.** A coroner observing "the agency has already implemented X" is not recommending X; if the finding states no further recommendation, it's a "no recommendation" case (excluded at sample time) — not MECH-OTHER.
- **Exhortations** ("I urge", "highlights the continued importance of") with no specific named action are MECH-OTHER plus SPEC-1 (aspirational).

## Axis 3 — Addressee

*Who is being directed to act?* Code the **most-specific named addressee**; do not infer.

| Code | Label | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| ADDR-DEPT-NAMED | Named government department | "NSW Health", "Department of Communities and Justice". |
| ADDR-DEPT-GENERIC | Government (generic) | "The government", "the State". |
| ADDR-HEALTH | Health service / LHD / hospital | A named health service or hospital. |
| ADDR-REG | Regulator | TGA, AHPRA, SafeWork, EPA, etc. |
| ADDR-PROF | Professional body / college | RACGP, ANMF, Law Society. |
| ADDR-POLICE | Police / corrections | Police force, Corrective Services. |
| ADDR-COURT | Coroner's court / judiciary | The coroner's court itself or other courts. |
| ADDR-PRIVATE | Private body | Named company, NGO, school. |
| ADDR-UNSPEC | Unspecified | No clear addressee. |

## Axis 4 — Specificity

*How actionable is the recommendation?* A 3-point ordinal scale. **SPEC-3 definition refined from v0**: was "specific, measurable, with deadline OR metric" (both seemed required); v1.0 makes the disjunction explicit and lowers the bar to be reachable in practice.

| Code | Label | Definition | Diagnostic |
|---|---|---|---|
| SPEC-3 | Specific & measurable | Names a specific action AND a named actor AND **either** a deadline / target date **or** a measurable outcome / target metric. Either of the two is sufficient. *Refined in v1.0.* | "By 1 July, X shall implement Y." OR "X shall reduce wait times below 30 minutes." |
| SPEC-2 | Specific (no metric or date) | Names a specific action AND a named actor but no deadline / outcome metric. | "X should review its policy on Y." |
| SPEC-1 | Aspirational | Names a goal or principle without binding any actor or action, OR is purely exhortative. | "Greater coordination across agencies is needed." "I urge ..." |

### Decision rules

- A recommendation that names *only the action* without an actor is SPEC-1 (no named actor to be held accountable).
- A recommendation that *quotes a deadline already in legislation* and re-applies it does count as a deadline for SPEC-3.
- "Should consider" / "is encouraged to" lowers SPEC by one band relative to "shall" / "must".

---

## Axis 5 — Scope

*Is the recommendation addressing the individual case or proposing systemic reform?*

| Code | Label | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| SCOPE-IND | Individual | Action localised to a single facility, patient, or family. |
| SCOPE-SYS | Systemic | Action intended to apply across a population, sector, or jurisdiction. |
| SCOPE-MIX | Mixed | Genuine span of both — retained for inter-rater work where two coders may legitimately disagree on IND/SYS. |

---

## Flags

In addition to the five axis labels, each row carries optional booleans:

- `ambiguous_<axis>` — coder was torn between two categories on this axis (60/40 = dominant; 51/49 = ambiguous).
- `requires_legal_expertise` — meaningful interpretation requires legal training.
- `non_english_or_redacted` — text contains substantial non-English content or redactions.

## Appendix — Unlabelled real-corpus snippets

These five real recommendations are deliberately **unlabelled** so they calibrate coders to prose style without anchoring labels.

> *(NSW)* "1. That the Office of Sport consider working with NSW Karate (and possibly other martial arts organisations) to develop a general head injury/concussion protocol or policy for the use of karate organisations at gradings and training. This process should take place with expert medical advice."

> *(WA)* "I recommend telephone calls recorded prior to the death of a prisoner in custody be provided to the coroners court as they were in the past. All prison deaths are mandated to be inquested, and while I appreciate not all telephone calls, although recorded are listened to within the prison system, nor is it feasible, they may provide some insight, post the event, as to what may have been in operation in the prisoner's mind at the time."

> *(ACT)* "I recommend that the ACT Government amend section 43 of the Health Act 1993 to avoid a repetition of this conduct."

> *(SA)* "Pursuant to Section 25(2) of the Coroner's Act 1975, I recommend that General Medical Practitioners be reminded that, when a patient has been referred to a specialist, it is necessary to communicate clearly with that specialist before prescribing medication or providing other forms of treatment which may be relevant to treatment being provided by the specialist."

> *(VIC)* "Pursuant to section 72(2) of the Coroners Act 2008, I make the following recommendation(s) connected with the death: 1. That the Public Advocate review OPA policy in relation to the scope of exercise of guardianship powers in particular in relation to the level of supervision and follow up of delivery of medical treatment."
